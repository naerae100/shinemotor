# Greenway Metal Recyclers — Information-Architecture Reference
Source: https://www.greenwaymetalrecyclers.com.au/ (fetched 2026-08-24). Raw HTML in `research/comp/`.
**Scope of this document: STRUCTURE ONLY.** Their visual design is a generic WordPress/Elementor template — rounded green cards, stock icons, gradient overlays, a slider hero. That is precisely what we are not doing. What is worth stealing is how they *sequence information* and how completely they answer a customer's questions.

## 2. Structural ideas worth adopting
- **Segment-targeted CTAs.** Nine audiences, each with its own quote button. Converts far better than one generic "Contact us" — a plumber sees themselves named on the page.
- **A real How It Works sequence.** Three steps, no more. It is genuinely a sequence, so numbering is honest rather than decorative.
- **FAQ that answers logistics, not marketing.** Their best questions are the mundane ones: *Do I need an appointment? What documentation do I need? Do you take contaminated scrap? Do you pick up? What are your hours?* Shine has answers to every one of these and currently publishes none of them.
- **Testimonials attributed by trade** ("Sarah R., Construction Contractor"), not just a first name — the occupation is what makes it persuasive to a peer.
- **Compliance stated up front rather than hidden.** Their bank-transfer notice is in the top bar. Shine's equivalent (no cash / EFT only, Scrap Metal Industry Regulations 2016) is currently buried in a bullet list and reads as a limitation. Framed correctly it is a *trust signal*: licensed, compliant, traceable.
- **Structured quote intake.** A "type of scrap" selector qualifies the lead before a human touches it. Shine's grade catalogue is far better material for this than Greenway's flat list.
- **Service-area as browsable content.** They publish 10 suburb pages. Shine covers all of NSW & ACT — a bigger claim, which should be made spatially rather than as a sentence.
- **Metals as a first-class content type**, each with its own page — not one flat icon grid.

## 4. What to reject outright
- **Slider heroes.** Two slides with identical body copy. Both sites do this. Neither should.
- **"Read More.." card teasers** that truncate a paragraph mid-sentence.
- **Repetition as a strategy.** "Request A Quote" appears 15+ times on one page; "top dollar" appears in nearly every block. Volume is not persuasion.
- **Filler ecology copy.** "In today's world, taking care of our environment is super important" — says nothing, costs trust.
- **Undifferentiated metal cards** — same icon treatment, same green, same shadow, for sixteen materials that look and sell nothing alike.
- **Nav dropdowns three levels deep.** Metals We Buy → Aluminium Recycle → Aluminium Radiators. Nobody navigates this.
- **Suburb-page doorway SEO.** Ten near-identical pages. Shine already has this disease in visible body copy; do not carry it into the new build.
- **Testimonials with no source.** Persuasive only if believable — needs the client to supply real ones.
