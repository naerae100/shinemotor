 FAQs
 Frequently Asked Questions
 Do I need an appointment to bring my scrap to your yard? 
 No appointment is necessary. You can visit our yard during our regular business hours 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, while on Saturday working hours are 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. to drop off your scrap materials.
 What if my scrap includes non-metallic items or contaminants? 
 While we prefer clean scrap materials, our experts can help assess and separate non-metallic items or contaminants. However, the value of your scrap may be affected.
 What documentation do I need to sell scrap materials? 
 You may need to provide identification, such as a driver’s license or ID card or proof of Ownership,
 to complete the transaction. Additionally, for large quantities, we may require additional paperwork.
 What types of scrap metals do you accept? 
 We accept a wide range of scrap metals, including copper, aluminum, brass, steel, lead, stainless steel, radiators, zinc, nickel, titanium, iron, bronze, cobalt, tungsten, tin, and chromium.
 Do you offer pickup services for large quantities of scrap? 
 Yes, we can arrange pickup services for larger quantities of scrap materials. Please contact us to schedule a pickup.
 What are your business hours for dropping off scrap materials? 
 Our business hours are 9 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. from Monday to Friday while on Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Please check our website or contact us via phone for any changes in our schedule.
