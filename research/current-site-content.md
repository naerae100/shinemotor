### 3.3 Buy From Us (`/buy-from-us.php`) — full grade catalogue
The most substantial page on the site. Uses real ISRI trade grade names — a genuine credibility asset that no competitor bothers with.

**Copper Scrap**
- **Mill Berry** — No. 1 bare, uncoated, unalloyed copper wire (Bare Bright). Wire gauge subject to buyer/seller agreement.
- **Candy** — clean, unalloyed, uncoated copper clippings, punchings, bus bars, commutator segments, clean copper tubing. Hydraulically briquetted copper available subject to agreement.
- **Birch Cliff** — combination of No. 2 copper wire and copper as defined in Birch and Cliff grades.
- **Ocean** — mixed automobile radiators, free of aluminium radiators and iron-finned radiators.

**Aluminium Scrap**
- **Extruded** — old 6063 extrusions.
- **Tense** — all types of clean aluminium castings, including automobile and aeroplane castings.
- **Talk** — clean aluminium/copper radiators, and aluminium fins on copper tubing.
- **Troma** — clean, single-piece, unplated aluminium wheels from auto and truck vehicles.

**Brass Scrap**
- **Honey** — mixed yellow brass solids, castings to tubing, including plated brass.
- **Night** — rod turnings, strictly free of aluminium, manganese, composition, Tobin and Muntz metal turnings; no more than 3% free iron.

**Stainless Steel**
- **Stainless Steel 316** — corrosion resistance and durability.
- **Stainless Steel 304** — versatile, durable.

**Ferrous Metal & HMS 1 & 2**
- **Ferrous Metal** — iron, steel, other ferrous alloys.
- **HMS 1 & 2** — quantity of each type depends on buyer/seller agreement.

**Wiring**
- **Insulated Copper Wires** — quality determined by buyer/seller agreement.
- **Druid** — copper wiring; copper recovery rate open to buyer/seller discussion.
- **Car Wiring Harness** — quality agreements tailored to both parties.

**Other**
- **Lead Scrap** — lead solids and shots, free of drosses and battery plates.
- **Electric Motors** — mixed electric motors.
- **Car Starter Motors / Alternators**
- **Compressors** — industrial through to household units.

> Note: every grade description on the live site ends with a near-identical "Trust Shine Motor Corporation to provide competitive prices and a seamless selling experience…" sentence. **This boilerplate must be cut** — the technical first sentence of each grade is the valuable part.

## 9. Problems with the current site (to fix, not repeat)
- Four-slide hero carousel where slides 3 and 4 are literally identical, and all four say "Welcome to Shine Motor Corporations".
- Page `<title>` on the blog is still **"Solor - Solar & Renewable Energy HTML Template"** — template never de-branded.
- Marquee strip of the same five words ("Brass Scrap / Stainless Steel / Ferrous Metal & HMS 1 & 2 / Wiring / Compressors") runs twice on every single page, in every footer.
- Gallery page has no gallery content in markup — only chrome.
- "Read More" links truncate the very copy the customer needs.
- Homepage "Our Services" section is an empty commented-out block — the three service pillars are never actually presented on the homepage.
- SEO keyword stuffing dumped into visible body copy on `/sell-your-scrap.php` ("scrap aluminium price sydney", "western sydney scrap metal", "metal recycling prices sydney").
- No FAQ, no how-it-works sequence, no testimonials, no visible service-area content, no quote form on the homepage.
- Two different landline numbers presented with no explanation ((02) 8712 6999 vs (02) 8712 9548) — **needs client confirmation**.
