 Welcome to Shine Motor Corporation
 Shine Motor Group is a worldwide market leader in collecting Ferrous and non Ferrous metals. We pay top price for all kinds of metals such as Copper, Brass, all types of radiators, Aluminium, Stainless steel, Steel, Batteries, Lead, and Electric Motors from the public, plumbers, electricians, manufacturers, demolition contractors, and tradies.
 We provide the best and highest market rates for your ferrous and non-ferrous scrap metals. We service all over NSW and ACT.

 Here at Shine Motor Corporation Pty Ltd, we strive to provide our customers with the best price for your scrap Copper, Brass, Copper cables, Stainless steel, Aluminium, electric motor and more. We offer best prices in the market with instant payment before you leave the site.
 The scrap is then processed, sorted and prepared at our facilities for sale to steel mills, brokers and exporters across the country and internationally.
 We are direct wholesalers from local market and export it to international markets, we deal with major shipping lines around the global.
